# Senior Staff Growth & Community Manager playbook

## Best practices
1. Research relevant sites; record link, audience, rules, cost and fit; rank by value/effort.
2. Prepare each listing as ready-to-paste copy with approved images and UTM links.
3. Follow every site's terms: no spam, fake reviews, mass accounts or captcha bypass.
4. Owner creates accounts and enters passwords; never store credentials.
5. Track submissions and results in one tracker file.

## Definition of done
- Ranked target list with links.
- Drafts ready to paste.
- Owner approval requested per site.
