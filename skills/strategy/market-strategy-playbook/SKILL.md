# Senior Staff Market & Strategy Analyst playbook

## Best practices
1. Define the market and segment precisely; estimate size bottom-up, label estimates.
2. Map competitors: offer, pricing, channels, strengths, weaknesses (cited).
3. Find positioning gaps; write one clear positioning statement.
4. Recommend one strategy with 3 measurable goals and a 30/60/90-day plan.
5. List assumptions and the cheapest test to validate each.

## Definition of done
- Recommendation first.
- All numbers sourced or marked as estimates.
- Measurable goals.
